Q&A

Questions people actually ask before they trust a credit app.

A straightforward FAQ block focused on trust and common decision-making questions.

How can I improve my credit score?

Pay on time, keep your credit card balance low, and don’t go on an application spree. Small habits, big changes.

Will checking my credit report affect my score?

Nope. Self-checks are soft inquiries. They’re like looking at your own report card — it doesn’t change the grade.

What is the normal range for a credit score?

300 to 900. Think of it this way: below 600 needs work, 700+ is solid, and 750+ is where lenders smile.

How often is my credit report updated?

Roughly once a month. Check in regularly so you’re never out of the loop.

What are common reasons for loan rejection?

Low score, high debt, weak income proof, or even small mistakes on your application — these trip people up all the time.

Is MeraCash safe and trustworthy?

100% secure. We use bank-grade encryption — not a marketing line, but real protection.

MeraCash (hereinafter referred to as "the App", "we" or "our company") highly values your privacy and information security. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and share your information when you use our App. Please read this policy carefully before using the App.

1. Introduction

MeraCash is a credit reporting and assessment app designed for users in India to help you understand your credit status and financial health. We are committed to respecting your privacy and strictly complying with relevant laws and regulations and Google Play policies.

This Privacy Policy applies to all information we collect during your use of MeraCash, including but not limited to device information, SMS data, installed app list, camera data, and location information. By using the App, you agree to all the terms set forth in this Privacy Policy.

We recommend that you review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information. If you have any questions about this policy, please feel free to contact us.

2. Information We Collect

SMS Permission: When you use the credit assessment or income analysis functions, we will request to read SMS messages related to financial transactions (including sender, time, and content). We will analyze this information using intelligent algorithms to improve the accuracy of credit assessment and repayment ability judgment. This information will be encrypted and uploaded to https://data.kalavritipri.com, deleted immediately after the assessment, and will not be stored or retained in any way, nor will it be disclosed to any third party. This permission is not mandatory; you can choose to refuse, and it will not affect the use of other functions.

Call Record Permission: If you choose to complete the registration or login process via voice verification, we will temporarily access and upload some of your recent call records to a secure server (https://data.kalavritipri.com) to ensure your SIM card can receive voice verification codes normally and to prevent potential fraud risks. Please rest assured that we will not collect all your call records and will do our best to protect your privacy and security.

Installed App List Permission: To further analyze your spending patterns and improve the accuracy of credit assessment, we will collect information on the financial applications installed on your device. This data will be encrypted and uploaded to our secure server (https://data.kalavritipri.com), and deleted immediately after analysis. We are committed to protecting your privacy; denying this permission will not affect the normal operation of the application, and you can decide whether to grant it.

Camera Permission: To facilitate your uploading of your personal bank credit report, we will request access to your device's camera. The uploaded files will be used to assess your income and credit status. These files will be encrypted and uploaded to our secure server (https://data.kalavritipri.com), and deleted immediately after the assessment. We are committed to always protecting your privacy and information security. This permission is not mandatory.

Location Permission: To better provide you with personalized services and enhance account security, we will collect your approximate location data. This information is mainly used for regional analysis and fraud prevention, and will be encrypted and uploaded to our secure server (https://data.kalavritipri.com). It will be deleted immediately after analysis. We fully respect your privacy; denying this permission will not affect the normal use of the application. You are free to choose whether to grant it.

Device Information Permission: To ensure the security and legality of our product use, we collect device-specific information, including SAID, IMEI, hardware model, and Android system version. This data is used for device identification and fraud prevention and will be encrypted and uploaded to our secure server (https://data.kalavritipri.com). We will never share your data with any third party without your explicit consent. Denying this permission will not affect the normal use of the application.
